2. The short version

Tintmap collects no personal data. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to any server of ours.

3. Pictures, paint boxes and settings

Tintmap analyses your photos on your device. Photos you open, the pictures you save, your paint boxes and your settings are stored inside the app on your device. They are not sent to us and we cannot see them. They are deleted when you delete them in the app or delete the app. Your device may include them in its own backups, as set by you; that is outside our control.

The app asks for access to the camera only when you choose to take a photo, and opens a photo from your library or your files only when you pick one. Nothing else in your library is read.

4. Purchase

The full version is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we receive no payment details and no name or e-mail address, only the confirmation that the purchase exists. Apple's privacy policy applies to the App Store.

5. App Store statistics

If you allow Apple to share app analytics with developers, Apple gives us statistics about installs, crashes and usage that do not identify you. We use them only to improve the app.

6. When you write to us

If you write to support, we use your e-mail address and your message to answer you (Art. 6 (1) (b) and (f) GDPR) and delete them when the matter is settled, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

7. This website

This website is hosted by Mittwald CM Service GmbH & Co. KG, Espelkamp, Germany, with whom a data processing agreement exists. When a page is requested, the server records the network address, time, page and browser for the period set by the host to keep the service secure (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). The website sets no cookies.

8. Your rights

You may ask what is stored about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a common format. You may also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen.
