# Account, Pro and your data

## Signing in

The web app asks you to sign in before it opens. There is no password: you enter your e-mail address and press **Send me a sign-in link**. A mail arrives with a link; open it on the same device and in the same browser, within 15 minutes, and you are signed in. If the mail does not come, look in your spam folder or press **Use different address** to start again.

New here? Choose **Sign up** under the form. Signing up works the same way, with one extra field: **Invitation code (optional)**. Without a code you get a free account; with a valid code your account starts with a lifetime licence. Each code works once.

### Two statements before you start

The first time you sign in, the app asks for two ticks before it opens:

1. that you have read and agree to the privacy policy and the terms,
2. that you understand where your work is stored: in this browser on this device, not in the cloud and not in your account.

The second one matters, and the rest of this chapter explains why. Press **Continue** and the app opens.

You stay signed in on this device for 60 days. **Sign out** in the account dialog ends it.

## Free, a picture, or Pro

The free version analyses your photos and gives you every recipe, mixed from the **Starter box**: twelve colours as a cheap student set has them. It shows you how Watercolourlove works with your own photos. To paint a picture with **your own paints**, unlock that picture with a credit, or go Pro for every picture.

| | Free | One picture (a credit) | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Analyse photos, mix and layer mode, colour list, recipes | yes | yes | yes |
| Paints to mix from | the Starter box | your own paints, in the boxes you have | your own paints, in as many boxes as you like |
| Blocking, close-ups, Simplify below 0 | | yes | yes |
| Save, Print / PDF, the template, Export as JPG | | yes | yes |
| More paint boxes, brand colour charts, importing a paint box | | | yes |
| Monochrome | | | yes |

The samples are open to everybody with everything a picture can have, so you can try each feature on them first.

When you reach a limit, the app tells you what would allow it and offers what fits: **Unlock this picture**, packs of pictures, or the plans. **Get pro** among the tools of the picture opens them at any time; beside it you see how many pictures you can still unlock.

### Pictures and credits

A credit unlocks **one picture for good**. Every account starts with one; more come in packs. They do not expire, and they stay when a subscription ends.

- **Unlock this picture** asks once more and shows how many you have left. Then the picture is yours: open it, change it, print it again, as often as you like. Close-ups and changed settings stay part of it.
- **The same photo is the same picture.** Opening the same file again, or the saved picture in the collection, costs nothing. Another photo is another picture. Pictures you unlocked carry the mark **Unlocked** in the collection.
- With a subscription or a lifetime licence you never spend a credit.

> **Note:** The photo still never leaves your device. To know a picture again, the app works out a fingerprint of the photo's file and sends only a key made from it and your account. Nobody can rebuild the photo from it, and the same photo in another account gives another key.

### Plans

| Plan | |
| --- | --- |
| **Monthly** | renews every month, cancel at any time |
| **Yearly** | renews every year, cancel at any time |
| **Lifetime licence** | one payment, no renewal |

The dialog shows the current prices. They include VAT. Payment is handled by Stripe; the app never sees your card. "Lifetime" means the life of the product: for as long as Watercolourlove is offered, not a promise that it runs forever. The terms explain this in full.

What you paid for pictures in the last 30 days comes off your first plan: the dialog says how much.

With a subscription you can **Switch to a lifetime licence** at any time. A **Voucher for a lifetime licence** is redeemed in the plan dialog with **Redeem**; a voucher only works with the e-mail address it was made out to.

### The account dialog

The person icon in the left column opens **Account**. It shows your address, your plan and how many pictures you can still unlock, and offers:

- **Manage subscription:** change or cancel a subscription, update the payment method.
- **Invoices:** your invoices, to view and download.
- **Download my data:** everything stored about your account, as a file.
- **Sign out.**
- **Delete account:** see below.

## Preferences

**Language** and **Appearance** are in the same dialog (without an account it is called **Preferences**).

![Language and appearance](shot:preferences)

- **Language:** English or German. All texts change at once, and the paints appear under their names in that language.
- **Appearance:** **Light**, **Dark** or **Automatic**, which follows the setting of your device. Dark is kind to the eyes in the evening; for judging colour, light is the safer choice, because the paper you paint on is white.

The preferences are stored in this browser, like everything you make in the app.

## Where your work lives

Watercolourlove does its work entirely on your device. Your photo is never uploaded: the analysis, the mixing and the layers are worked out in your browser. What you keep, you keep there too:

- the pictures in your collection, with their settings and colour changes,
- your paint boxes and the colours of your own,
- your preferences and the arrangement of the panels.

None of this is part of your account. That has three consequences you should know:

1. **Another device is a fresh start.** Sign in on your tablet and you have your plan there, but not your collection or your paint boxes.
2. **Clearing the browser's data deletes your work.** "Clear browsing data", "Delete cookies and site data" or a private window that you close all remove it, and nobody can bring it back.
3. **Your work is private by design.** Nobody, including us, can see your photos or your paints.

> **Tip:** Back up your paint boxes. In the paint box, **Export** under each box saves it as a file; **Import paint box** brings it back, on this device or another. A paint box with your own calibrated colours is worth keeping. See [Paint box and palette](paint-box.html).

### Deleting your account

**Delete account** removes your account for good. A subscription ends at once; a lifetime licence is lost. What is saved in this browser stays: the app asks you to confirm, and your pictures and paint boxes are still there afterwards, until you clear them yourself.

## Cookies and statistics

The app needs two small cookies to keep you signed in. With your permission it also counts visits with Google Analytics, to learn which parts of the app are used. A banner asks the first time you open the app:

- **Accept all** allows the statistics,
- **Reject all** allows only what is needed to sign in,
- **Settings** shows each category, with **Save choice**.

Without your consent, nothing is requested from Google. You can change your choice at any time with **Cookies** at the bottom of the page; the app asks again after 13 months. Your photos are never part of any statistics.

## The apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Watercolourlove will also come as an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, with the same analysis, recipes and layers. The apps need no account: everything stays on the device, and a single purchase unlocks the full version, with no subscription. Your work in the browser and in the apps is kept separately; paint boxes can be moved between them as exported files.
