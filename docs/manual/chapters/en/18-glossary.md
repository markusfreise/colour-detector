# Glossary

## Labels and numbers

What you read on the picture, in the colour list and in the recipes.

| Label | German | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 6L, 6R | 6L, 6R | Mix 6, painted as a light wash (L) or a rich wash (R). The number is the mix, the letter the amount of water. |
| W | W, sehr wässrig | Very watery: hardly more than tinted water. |
| L | L, leicht | Light wash. |
| M | M, mittel | Medium wash. |
| R | R, kräftig | Rich wash: little water, strong colour. |
| C | C, konzentriert | Concentrated: paint that hardly flows. |
| W1 to C5 | W1 bis C5 | In values mode, finer steps of water within each level. |
| A1, A2 | A1, A2 | Layer mode: accents, numbered in the order they are painted, last of all. |
| 1, 2 (on a layer) | 1, 2 | Layer mode, one layer shown on its own: the number of coats an area gets. |
| P, T, P/T | P, T, P/T | Pan, tube, or both: the forms in which you own a paint. |
| 3 x P 672 | 3 x P 672 | Three parts of paint 672 from the pan. Parts are counted in brush loads. |
| 1:9 with water | 1:9 mit Wasser | Layer mode: one part paint to nine parts water, with a comparison such as "like coffee". |
| 0.8 | 0,8 | The distance between target and mix, in steps the eye can just tell apart (see Match quality). |
| 52 in 20 mixes | 52 in 20 Mischungen | 52 colours on the paper, made from 20 puddles in different washes. |

## In the app

| Term | German | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Accent | Akzent | Layer mode: a strong wash on small areas, painted last, first for a colour the layers miss, then for the darks. |
| Auto | Auto | Lets the app choose the paints that suit the picture, from the boxes ticked **Use for Auto**. |
| Base paint | Grundfarbe | Layer mode: a paint that gets a layer of its own. |
| Coat | Lasur (Durchgang) | Layer mode: one pass of a layer's wash. An area can get the same wash once, twice or three times. |
| Colour area | Farbfläche | A shape of the picture that gets one wash of one mix. |
| Colours | Farben | The setting for the number of mixes, and the list of them. |
| Detail | Ausschnitt | A part of the picture analysed as a picture of its own. Pro. |
| Details | Details | The setting that decides whether small areas of another hue keep a mix of their own. |
| Full breakdown | Alle Details | Target and mix side by side, the colour by hand and every recipe of a colour. |
| Geometric | Geometrisch | Areas as polygons with straight edges instead of their organic outline. |
| Layer | Schicht | Layer mode: the wash of one base paint, painted over the dry layers before it. |
| Layer mode | Schichten | The method of glazing a few washes over each other instead of mixing every colour. |
| Layered paint | Geschichtete Farbe | Layer mode: the view of the finished picture, all layers together. |
| Match quality | Treffer | How close a recipe gets to its target: excellent (below 2), good (below 5), fair (below 10), rough (10 and above). German: sehr gut, gut, mäßig, grob. |
| Max. coats | Max. Lasuren | Layer mode: how often one wash may be painted over itself. |
| Max. paints | Max. Farben | How many paints a mix may have: one, two or three. |
| Mix | Mischung | One puddle of paint on the palette, with a number. It can be painted in several washes. |
| Mixed paint | Gemischte Farbe | The view of what your paints can reach. |
| Organic | Organisch | Areas that follow the outlines of the photo. |
| Overlay | Überlagerung | The view of the photo at half strength over the paint. |
| Paint box | Farbkasten | A named set of paints you own, for example your pans or a travel set. |
| Palette | Palette | The paints a picture is mixed from. It is saved with the picture. |
| Photo colour | Fotofarbe | The view of each area in the average colour of the photo there. |
| Recipe | Rezept | Which paints, in how many parts, with how much water, for one colour. |
| Simplify areas | Flächen vereinfachen | Merges small shapes into larger, calmer ones. |
| Target | Ziel | The colour the photo asks for in an area. |
| Template to print | Vorlage zum Drucken | Only outlines and numbers, in light blue, to print and paint on. |
| Values | Tonwerte | The picture analysed in grey; each value is painted with one paint of the palette. |
| Wash | Lasur | A mix at one water level. |
| Your mix | Deine Mischung | The colour the recipe gives on white paper. |

## Painting terms

| Term | German | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Backrun, bloom | Blumenkohlrand | A frilled edge where wetter paint runs back into a wash that has begun to dry. |
| Cold-pressed paper, NOT | Matt, kaltgepresst | Paper with a medium grain, the usual choice for watercolour. |
| Complementary colours | Komplementärfarben | Colours opposite each other on the colour wheel; mixed, they neutralise each other. |
| Edge, hard, soft, lost | Kante, hart, weich, verloren | The boundary of a shape: painted on dry paper, softened, or dissolved into its neighbour. |
| Glaze | Lasur | A thin transparent wash over a dry one. |
| Granulation | Granulation | Pigment particles that settle into the grain of the paper and give texture. |
| Hot-pressed paper | Satiniert, heißgepresst | Smooth paper, good for fine detail, harder for even washes. |
| Lifting | Auswaschen, Anlösen | Taking paint off the paper with a damp brush or tissue; also the unwanted disturbing of a dry layer by the next one. |
| Masstone | Purton | The colour of a paint at full strength. |
| Mud | Schmutz | A dull, lifeless mix, usually from too many paints or from working into a drying wash. |
| Opaque | Deckend | A paint that covers what is below it rather than filtering it. |
| Rough paper | Rau, Torchon | Paper with a strong grain. |
| Sizing | Leimung | The gelatine or other size in and on the paper that keeps paint from sinking in like ink in blotting paper. |
| Staining | Färbend | A paint that bites into the paper fibres and can hardly be lifted once dry. |
| Transparent | Transparent, lasierend | A paint that lets light through to the paper. |
| Undertone | Unterton | The colour of a paint in a thin wash, which can differ from its masstone. |
| Value | Tonwert | How light or dark a colour is, regardless of its hue. |
| Wet-in-wet | Nass in nass | Paint laid into a wash or onto paper that is still wet, so it spreads softly. |
| Wet-on-dry | Nass auf trocken | Paint laid onto dry paper, for crisp edges. |
