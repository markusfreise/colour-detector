# Troubleshooting

## The picture

### The map has far too many tiny areas

Raise **Simplify areas**. Every step merges small shapes into their neighbours; around the middle of the range most photos become paintable. Lowering **Colours** helps too, because fewer mixes mean fewer boundaries between them. See [Colour areas](colour-areas.html).

### An important detail has disappeared

Small areas that differ in hue from their surroundings, a red roof, the lips in a portrait, keep a mix of their own when **Details** is set high. If the detail is also small in size, simplifying merges it away; look at that part of the picture on its own with **Detail**, where it gets finer areas and mixes of its own (Pro). See [Pictures](pictures.html).

### The colours look dull, or far too loud

The app raises **Saturation** to 200% before it analyses the photo, because photos usually look flatter than the place did. For a photo that is already strongly coloured, lower it towards 100%. **Contrast** works the same way for the difference between light and dark. Both change only the analysis, never the photo itself.

## The recipes

### A colour only gets "fair" or "rough"

The paints in your palette cannot reach that colour closely. Compare the views **Photo colour** and **Mixed paint**: if the difference does not bother you, leave it. Otherwise:

- allow three paints per mix with **Max. paints**,
- put a paint into the palette that lies closer to the colour, often a cleaner yellow, red or blue,
- or change the target in **Full breakdown**: you are the painter, and a slightly different colour that you can mix cleanly is often better than the exact one in mud.

### A recipe uses a paint I do not want

Open **Full breakdown** and choose one of the other recipes. If you never want that paint in this picture, take it out of the palette in the **Paint box** and press **Update mixes**.

### A dark colour cannot be reached

No paint is used darker than its recorded colour, its swatch. A dark that lies beyond every single paint needs a mix of two or three. Allow more paints per mix, add a dark paint such as indigo, sepia or a neutral tint to the palette, or in layer mode let the darks come from several layers. If the darks are always a little too light, the recorded colours of your dark paints may be lighter than your real paints: see [Calibrating your paints](calibrating.html).

### My paint is not in the paint box

Press the **+** at the end of a box. Search by series, number or name; with Pro the colour charts of Schmincke Horadam, Winsor & Newton Professional and Michael Harding are searched too. If your paint is not there, choose **Or enter a colour of your own**. See [Paint box and palette](paint-box.html).

## Painting

### The mix looks different when it is dry

Watercolour dries lighter, and often a little cooler and duller, than it looks wet. Always judge a test stroke on a scrap of the same paper after it has dried completely. If it is too pale, add paint; if it is too strong, add water.

### My colours turn muddy

Mud has three usual causes: too many paints in one mix, working back into a wash that has started to dry, and painting over a layer that is not completely dry. Keep mixes to two or three paints, paint each area in one pass, and wait. See [Mixing in practice](mixing.html).

### Frilled edges appear in my washes

Those are backruns, or blooms: wetter paint has run back into a wash that had begun to dry. Either work while the whole area is evenly wet, or wait until it is completely dry. A damp, not wet, brush picks up excess water at the edge of a wash. See [Washes](washes.html).

### A glaze lifted the layer below

The layer below was not quite dry, or the brush went over it more than once. Let every layer dry fully, use a soft brush, carry the paint and do not scrub. For first layers, staining paints are safest. See [Glazing](glazing.html).

## The app

### I changed the paints, but nothing happened

Changes in the paint box take effect when you press **Update mixes**. Settings such as **Colours** or **Simplify areas** analyse the picture again as soon as you let go of the slider.

### A saved picture has disappeared

Pictures, paint boxes and the collection are stored in the browser on this device, not in your account and not in a cloud. Clearing the browser's data, using a private window or switching to another browser or device means they are not there. Keep the pictures that matter to you as a PDF with **Print / PDF**.

### I cannot save, print or export

In the free version on the web, pictures cannot be saved, printed or exported, and the palette mixes from the Starter box instead of your own paints. Unlocking the picture with a credit lifts these limits for that picture; Pro lifts them for every picture. See [Account, Pro and your data](account.html).

### The sign-in link does not work

A sign-in link works once and for 15 minutes, on the device where you asked for it. If it has expired or was already used, ask for a new one on the sign-in page. If no mail arrives, look in the spam folder.

### Something else

Write to [support@markus-freise.de](mailto:support@markus-freise.de). Say what you did, what you expected and what happened instead; a screenshot helps.
