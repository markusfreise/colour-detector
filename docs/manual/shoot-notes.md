# Notes for photographing the painting process

The photos of the manual show hands, paper, paint and the finished picture; the app itself is shown
by screenshots, taken automatically. `photos.md` lists every photo still missing, with what it has
to show.

- **Format:** landscape 3:2, about 2400 px wide, saved as WebP or JPG under
  `public/manual/photos/<name>.webp`. The page crops nothing but shows the photo at the width of
  the text.
- **Light:** a window from the side opposite the painting hand, a white card as fill. No lamp mixed
  in, or the paint colours shift. No flash.
- **One fixed top-down camera** for everything painted on the board: mark the board's place on the
  desk with tape, so the stages of one painting can be compared from photo to photo.
- **Screens:** do not photograph the app; where a photo shows a device, it is only for context.
  Filmed or photographed screens flicker and the text is too small.
- **Which picture:** the screenshots show the sample "Llyn Idwal". Painting that motif for the
  photos keeps the manual one story: the printed sheet, the mixes and the labels in the photos are
  then the real ones.
- **Honesty:** a test strip that is off, then corrected, teaches more than a perfect one.
