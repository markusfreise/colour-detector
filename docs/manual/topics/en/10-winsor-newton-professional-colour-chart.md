# Which Winsor & Newton Professional colours do I need, and how do I read the chart?

Winsor & Newton Professional is the range many painters meet first in an art shop, and its chart is long: 116 colours, several yellows and reds in two or three versions each. Most of it you will never need. The chart is the tool for finding the few colours you do.

Watercolourlove is not affiliated with Winsor & Newton. The swatches on this page are the reference colours the app uses, read from the maker's own published chart; that chart and the paint on your desk have the last word.

## The range

The chart in the app has 116 colours, in the order of the maker's chart: yellows, oranges, reds, rose and violets, blues, turquoises, greens, earths, greys and blacks, and the two whites last. Each colour has a three-digit number, the one on the tube and the half pan; leading zeros count, as in **074 Burnt Sienna**.

Two things stand out in this range. Many colours come as a pair, a cadmium and a cadmium-free version side by side, such as **094 Cadmium Red** and **901 Cadmium-Free Red**. And the "Winsor" colours, such as **730 Winsor Yellow** or **707 Winsor Blue (Green Shade)**, are the maker's own names for modern, strong pigments; the chart tells you which.

## How to read a maker's chart

Every maker prints the same handful of facts for each colour, each in its own symbols, explained in the key of the chart.

- **Number and name.** The number identifies the paint; names change between languages and editions.
- **Pigments.** The Colour Index names, such as PB29 for ultramarine. A paint of one pigment mixes more cleanly than one that is already a blend.
- **Transparency.** Usually a square, empty for transparent and filled for opaque. Transparent paints glaze; opaque ones cover a little.
- **Staining.** Whether the paint lifts once dry. Staining paints make good first layers.
- **Granulation.** Whether the pigment settles into the grain of the paper.
- **Lightfastness or permanence.** How well the colour lasts in daylight. For work you want to keep, choose the highest ratings.
- **Series.** The price group; the cadmiums and cobalts are in the higher series.

## Choosing a small palette

Six colours carry most landscapes and street scenes. From this range that could be a cool and a warm yellow, **722 Winsor Lemon** and **730 Winsor Yellow**; two blues, **263 French Ultramarine** (PB29) and **707 Winsor Blue (Green Shade)** (PB15:3); a cool red, **502 Permanent Rose** (PV19); and the earths **552 Raw Sienna** and **074 Burnt Sienna**. Ultramarine and burnt sienna alone give every grey from blue to brown.

The phthalo blue is strong: add it to a mix in small amounts. If you prefer to avoid cadmium, the cadmium-free versions sit right next to the cadmiums on the chart. And before you buy a convenience colour such as a sap green or a Payne's grey, check its pigments: it is often a mix of colours already in your box.

Swatch every new paint on your own paper, strong and thin. The chapter [Paint box and palette](paint-box.html) goes into how many paints make a good palette, and [Mixing watercolours](mixing-watercolours.html) into making two of them reach a colour.

## How the colours in the app were read

For 48 colours the maker's online chart shows a flat swatch, and the app takes that colour as shown. The others were sampled from the printed chart's brushed swatches, at their darker end, because that end matches the flat swatches best. The app treats each value as the paint at strength 1, the darkest it is used in a recipe, and as a starting point rather than a measurement.

So the colours on your screen are approximations: of a printed swatch, on someone else's paper, shown by your screen. If a paint keeps mixing differently from what the app expects, [calibrate it](calibrating.html) from a swatch of your own, and the recipes follow your paint.

## Questions and answers

### How many colours does Winsor & Newton Professional Watercolour have?

The chart in Watercolourlove has 116 colours. Makers add and retire colours over the years, so the current chart of Winsor & Newton has the last word.

### What is the difference between Cadmium Red and Cadmium-Free Red?

The cadmium-free colours are made with other pigments to come close to the cadmiums in hue and strength. They sit side by side on the chart; swatch both if the difference matters to you.

### Are the colours on this page exact?

No. They are read from the maker's chart, in places from brushed swatches, and every screen shows them a little differently. Swatch your own paint to be sure, and calibrate it in the app if it differs.

### Can Watercolourlove mix from my Winsor & Newton paints?

Yes. With Pro you add them from the chart by name, number or pigment. With a picture you unlock, you can enter them yourself, or use the common colours of the library.
