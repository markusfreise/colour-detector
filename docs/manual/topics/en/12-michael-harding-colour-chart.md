# Which Michael Harding watercolours do I need, and how do I read the chart?

The Michael Harding range is a newer one among the large artists' watercolours, and a wide one: 136 colours. What sets it apart on the chart is the earths. Next to burnt sienna and the umbers stand ochres and reds named for where they come from, such as **W122 Pozzuoli Red**, **W158 Puisaye Hematite** or **W157 Earth of Cyprus**. For a landscape painter that is a box of quiet, natural colour; for choosing a palette, it is a lot to sort through.

Watercolourlove is not affiliated with Michael Harding. The swatches on this page are the reference colours the app uses, read from the maker's own published swatches; those and the paint on your desk have the last word.

## The range

The chart in the app has 136 colours, in the maker's order: whites, yellows, oranges, reds, rose and violets, blues, turquoises, greens, the long row of yellow and red earths and browns, then greys and blacks. Each colour has a number that starts with **W**, such as **W201 Ultramarine Blue**, printed on the tube. Type it with the W when you search.

Several hues come in more than one version. There are cadmiums and their modern alternatives, a lemon yellow and a Hansa yellow, a Phthalocyanine Blue Lake and a Phthalo Blue Red Shade. The chart's pigment information tells you which of them are really different paints.

## How to read a maker's chart

Every maker prints the same handful of facts for each colour, each in its own symbols, explained in the key of the chart.

- **Number and name.** The number identifies the paint; names change between languages and editions.
- **Pigments.** The Colour Index names, such as PB29 for ultramarine or PBr7 for natural siennas and umbers. A paint of one pigment mixes more cleanly than a blend.
- **Transparency.** Transparent paints glaze cleanly; opaque ones cover a little. Many natural earths are on the opaque side.
- **Staining.** Whether the paint lifts once dry. Staining paints make good first layers.
- **Granulation.** Whether the pigment settles into the grain of the paper. Many earths and the cobalts granulate, which suits stone and foliage.
- **Lightfastness.** How well the colour holds in daylight. For work you want to keep, choose the highest ratings.

## Choosing a small palette

A small palette from this range could be a cool and a warm yellow, **W108 Lemon Yellow** and **W231 Hansa Yellow Medium**; a warm and a cool blue, **W201 Ultramarine Blue** and **W139 Phthalo Blue Red Shade**; a cool red, **W211 Quinacridone Rose**; and two earths, **W119 Yellow Ochre** and **W125 Burnt Sienna**. Those seven reach greens, oranges, violets and the neutral greys of ultramarine with burnt sienna.

Then let the earths do what they do best. Instead of five more earths, choose one that your subjects keep asking for: a red earth for brick and roof tiles, a green earth for distant hills. Check its pigment on the chart against the ones you have: two earths of the same pigment often look alike in a mix.

## How the colours in the app were read

Michael Harding shows each colour as a photo of a painted brushstroke. The app takes the median colour of the strong end of each stroke, its left third, and treats it as the paint at strength 1, the darkest it is used in a recipe. That makes every value a starting point rather than a measurement: a photo of a swatch, on the maker's paper and in the maker's light, shown by your screen.

If a paint keeps mixing differently from what the app expects, [calibrate it](calibrating.html) from a swatch of your own, and the recipes follow your paint.

## Questions and answers

### How many colours does Michael Harding Artists Watercolour have?

The range in Watercolourlove has 136 colours, as the maker's website listed them in October 2026. Makers add and retire colours, so the current list has the last word.

### Why do Michael Harding numbers start with W?

The W marks the watercolours in the maker's numbering. Search for the number with the W, such as "W201".

### Are the colours on this page exact?

No. They are read from photos of painted swatches on the maker's website, and every screen shows them a little differently. Swatch your own paint to be sure, and calibrate it in the app if it differs.

### Can Watercolourlove mix from my Michael Harding paints?

Yes. With Pro you add them from the chart by name or number. With a picture you unlock, you can enter them yourself, or use the common colours of the library.
