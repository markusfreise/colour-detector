The app that shows you how to mix the right watercolours for your pictures

Watercolourlove turns a photo into a plan for a real watercolour. It finds the colours that matter, tells you how to mix each one from the paints you own, and draws the photo as flat, numbered areas you can transfer to paper.

Open the app Read the manual

How it works

  1. Add a reference photo for your picture.
  2. Add your watercolours: the paints you actually own.
  3. Let the app analyse the mixes: every colour area gets a number and a recipe, with how much water to use.

Every painter who works from a photo faces the same two questions. Which colours are really in this picture, once you look past the thousand small variations a camera records? And how do I mix each of them from the paints in my box? Watercolourlove answers both. The result is a paint-by-numbers plan made for watercolour: it knows that paint is transparent, that water makes a colour lighter, and that the white of the paper is the lightest thing you have. The quick start takes one picture from the first click to the last brush stroke.

Two ways to paint

Mix. Every colour of the picture is mixed on the palette, and each area is painted once with its mix, at up to five strengths of water. Twenty mixes give you far more than twenty colours on paper. See mixes and recipes.

Layer. A few base paints are glazed over each other, light to dark. Greens, oranges and darks appear where the layers overlap: classic glazing, planned for you step by step. See layer mode.

Your paints

The paint box knows 40 common colours by name and pigment, and with Pro the colour charts of Schmincke Horadam, Winsor & Newton Professional, Michael Harding and Sennelier L'Aquarelle. Any other paint goes in as a colour of your own, and you can calibrate the reference colours to your own swatches.

Your pictures stay with you

Everything is worked out on your device. The photo is never uploaded; your pictures, paint boxes and collection stay in your browser. Your account is only your e-mail address.

What it costs

Signing up is free and comes with a starter box of twelve colours, the sample photos and one picture of your own. Picture packs and Pro unlock your own paints and every feature. See the prices.
