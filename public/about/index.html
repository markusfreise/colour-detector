The app that shows you how to mix the right watercolours for your pictures

A tool for painters who work from photos. Watercolourlove finds the colours in your reference that matter and works out how to mix each one from the paints you own, with how much water. Made for professionals and ambitious amateurs who want to see a picture's colours clearly before they paint it.

Seeing the colours

Every painter who works from a photo faces the same two questions. Which colours are really in this picture, once you look past the thousand small variations a camera records? And how do I mix each of them from the paints in my box? Watercolourlove answers both: it identifies the colours that carry the picture, groups them into a manageable number of mixes, and shows you where in the picture each one sits.

Mixing them

For every mix the app works out a recipe from your own paints: which paints, in what ratio, at which strength of water. It knows that watercolour is transparent, that water makes a colour lighter, and that a paint cannot get darker than it is at full strength. You choose between the recipes, and a test strip on your paper has the last word. See mixes, washes and recipes and mixing in practice.

Reading the reference

  • Colour areas: how many mixes, how simple the shapes, and which small but distinct colours keep a mix of their own. See colour areas.
  • Blocking and values: light and shadow as two or three flat tones, to find the large forms first; monochrome as a study in light and dark. See values.
  • Layer mode: a few base paints glazed over each other, planned from light to dark. See layer mode.
  • Close-ups of one part of the reference, analysed on their own. See pictures.

Aids at the desk

When you want them: a printout or PDF with every mix, a grid to transfer the drawing, numbered areas, an export as JPG. See printing and exporting.

Your paints

The paint box knows 40 common colours by name and pigment, and with Pro the colour charts of Schmincke Horadam, Winsor & Newton Professional, Michael Harding and Sennelier L'Aquarelle. Any other paint goes in as a colour of your own, and you can calibrate the reference colours to your own swatches.

Your pictures stay with you

Everything is worked out on your device. The photo is never uploaded; your pictures, paint boxes and collection stay in your browser. Your account is only your e-mail address.

What it costs

Signing up is free and comes with a starter box of twelve colours, the sample photos and one picture of your own. Picture packs and Pro unlock your own paints and every feature. See the prices.
