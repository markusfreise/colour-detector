Watercolourlove on the web

Watercolourlove for iPhone, iPad and Mac

The app looks at a photo, finds its main colours and tells you how to mix every one of them from your own watercolours. The photo becomes a calm map of flat, numbered areas; tap one to see which paints to combine, in what ratio and with how much water.

Everything is worked out on your device: no account, no advertising, no tracking.
