Watercolourlove app

Privacy policy

Version 2026-10. This policy is for the app from the App Store. The website watercolourlove.de has its own privacy policy.

Draft. This text has not been checked by a lawyer yet.

1. Who is responsible

Markus Freise, Weststraße 48, 33615 Bielefeld, Germany. E-mail: support@markus-freise.de. Details are in the imprint.

2. The short version

The Watercolourlove app for iPhone, iPad and Mac needs no account and collects no personal data as long as you do not sign in. It has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Signing in to a watercolourlove.de account is optional; only then does the app connect to our server, and only your e-mail address and your plan are exchanged. Your pictures never leave your device.

3. Pictures, paint boxes and settings

The app analyses your photos on your device. Photos you open, the pictures you save, your paint boxes and your settings are stored inside the app on your device. They are not sent to us and we cannot see them. They are deleted when you delete them in the app or delete the app. Your device may include them in its own backups, as set by you; that is outside our control.

The app asks for access to the camera only when you choose to take a photo, and opens a photo from your library or your files only when you pick one. Nothing else in your library is read.

4. Purchase

The full version is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we receive no payment details and no name or e-mail address, only the confirmation that the purchase exists. Apple's privacy policy applies to the App Store.

5. Optional account

If you already have a plan on watercolourlove.de, you can sign in to that account in the app so that the plan unlocks the app too. You sign in with Apple or with Google. We then receive from Apple or Google your verified e-mail address (with Apple, possibly a relay address that Apple forwards to you) and an identifier for your sign-in; nothing else, no name and no contacts. On our server we store your e-mail address, that identifier, your plan and when you agreed to the privacy policy. The app keeps a sign-in token in your device's keychain and asks our server for your plan when it starts. We use these data only to provide the account (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR).

You can sign out at any time in the app's settings. You can also delete the account there: we then delete your account and everything stored with it, and withdraw the app's access to your Apple sign-in. Details about the account are in the privacy policy of the website.

6. App Store statistics

If you allow Apple to share app analytics with developers, Apple gives us statistics about installs, crashes and usage that do not identify you. We use them only to improve the app.

7. When you write to us

If you write to support, we use your e-mail address and your message to answer you (Art. 6 (1) (b) and (f) GDPR) and delete them when the matter is settled, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

8. This website

This website is hosted by Mittwald CM Service GmbH & Co. KG, Espelkamp, Germany, with whom a data processing agreement exists. When a page is requested, the server records the network address, time, page and browser for the period set by the host to keep the service secure (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). The website sets no cookies.

9. Your rights

You may ask what is stored about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a common format. You may also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen.
