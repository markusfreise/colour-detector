Support

Questions, ideas or a recipe that does not look right? Write to support@markus-freise.de. Please say which device and version of the app you use (Settings, or the About window on the Mac); a screenshot helps.

Common questions

Where are my pictures? Only on your device, inside the app. They are not uploaded anywhere. If you delete the app, they are deleted with it.

I bought the full version on another device. Open the purchase screen and choose "Restore purchase". It works on every device signed in with the same Apple Account.

How exact are the recipes? They come from a model of how transparent paint behaves on white paper. Real paint varies with pigment, paper and water, so treat every recipe as a starting point and test it on a scrap first.

My paints are not in the list. Add them in the paint box with their own colour. Paints of any brand work.
