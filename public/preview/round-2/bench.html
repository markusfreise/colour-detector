Method Plan Shapes Photo

Method

Mix: every area gets one mixed colour, painted once. Layer: a few thin washes, each painted over the dry one before.

Method

Plan

Colours sets how many mixed colours the plan has — more means more detail and more mixes to prepare. Variety at 0 keeps the picture as close to the photo as it can; at 10 a small area of another hue may keep its own mix. Max. paints is how many paints one mix may use, Max. coats how often one wash may be painted over itself. Layers and Accents only apply in layer mode: accents are small strong touches painted last. Colours you changed by hand are carried over when the plan is worked out again, as long as the colour is still in the plan.
