Privacy policy

1. Who is responsible

Markus Freise, street and number, postcode and town, Germany. E-mail: support@markus-freise.de. Details are in the imprint.

2. The short version

  • Your pictures are worked on in your browser. They are not uploaded to us and we cannot see them.
  • Pictures you save, your paint boxes and your settings are stored in your browser on your device, not in your account and not in a cloud.
  • An account consists of your e-mail address, the consent you gave and whether you have paid. There is no profile.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your card details.

3. Pictures and other content

The app analyses a photo with code that runs in your browser. The photo, the colour areas, the recipes, your saved collection and your paint boxes are kept in the storage of your browser (local storage and IndexedDB). They stay there until you delete them or clear the data of your browser. We have no copy and cannot restore them.

You are responsible for the pictures you open. Only use pictures you are allowed to use.

4. The optional AI help

The AI help is switched off unless you switch it on. It uses your own key for a service of Anthropic or OpenAI. When you press the button, a reduced copy of the picture, without file data such as place or date, and the names and colours of your paints are sent from your browser straight to that service, under your account there and its terms. Nothing passes through our server. Your key is kept in your browser, and only if you tick "Remember".

5. Your account

To sign in we store:

  • your e-mail address,
  • if you sign in with Google, Apple or Facebook: the identifier that service gives us for you, and nothing else from your profile there,
  • the date and version of the consent you gave,
  • whether you have a subscription or a lifetime licence, until when it is paid, and your customer number at Stripe,
  • a session, so that you stay signed in for up to 60 days.

Legal basis: performance of the contract with you (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR). When you sign in with Google, Apple or Facebook, that service learns that you use this app; its own privacy policy applies. A sign-in link by e-mail is valid for 15 minutes. To keep the sign-in from being abused we count requests per e-mail address and network address for one hour, in a form from which the address cannot be read (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

6. Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in, and a short-lived one protects the sign-in with Google, Apple or Facebook. Both are necessary for the service and are not used for tracking. If analytics are switched on: Google Analytics is used only after you agree in the banner, and you can change your choice under "Privacy settings".

7. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., Ireland. Stripe receives your e-mail address and the payment data you enter on its pages. We receive the result of the payment and your customer number. Invoices are kept as long as tax law requires, ten years in Germany (Art. 6 (1) (c) GDPR).

8. Hosting and server logs

The app and the account service run at Mittwald CM Service GmbH & Co. KG, Espelkamp, Germany, with whom a data processing agreement exists. When a page is requested, the server records the network address, time, page and browser for the period set by the host to keep the service secure (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

9. Who else receives data

  • The host named above.
  • Stripe, for payments. Stripe may process data outside the EU on the basis of the EU standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Google, Apple or Meta, only if you choose to sign in with them.

We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.

10. How long data is kept

Account data is kept until you delete your account. You can do that yourself at any time under "Account"; it takes effect at once and ends a running subscription. Sessions end after 60 days, sign-in links after 15 minutes. Invoices stay with Stripe and in our books for the period the law requires.

11. Your rights

You may ask what is stored about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a common format. Under "Account" you can download everything stored about your account and delete the account. You may withdraw a consent at any time for the future. You may also complain to a data protection authority, for example the authority of the federal state where the operator lives.

12. Changes

When this policy changes in a way that matters, the app asks you to read and confirm the new version.
