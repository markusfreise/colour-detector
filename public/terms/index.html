1. What this is

Watercolourlove is a web app by Markus Freise (see imprint). It finds the main colours in a photo, proposes how to mix them from your watercolours and shows the photo as flat, numbered areas. These terms apply between you and Markus Freise when you create an account.

2. Account

You need an account to use the app. It consists of your e-mail address. You can delete it at any time under "Account".

3. Your pictures stay with you

Pictures, paint boxes and saved collections are stored in your browser on your device. They are not stored on our server and are not part of your account. If you clear the data of your browser, change browser or device, or lose the device, they are gone and we cannot bring them back. Print or export what you want to keep.

You confirm that you are allowed to use the pictures you open in the app. You remain responsible for them and for what you make from them.

A subscription renews by its period until you cancel it. You can cancel at any time under "Account", "Manage subscription", with effect at the end of the period already paid.

If the app is discontinued, we announce it three months ahead where that is possible. If the app stops being available within 24 months of your purchase, you get back the part of the price for the months that are missing from that period. After that period there is no refund when the app ends.
